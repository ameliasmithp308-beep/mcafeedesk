Frequently Asked Questions & Troubleshooting

Q: What if I get an "Invalid Product Key" error?

Check for commonly mistaken characters (such as confusing the letter "O" with number "0", or letter "I" with number "1"). Ensure no spaces are entered before or after the key.

Q: Should I uninstall previous antivirus software?

Yes. Having two active antivirus programs running simultaneously can cause system crashes or slowness. Please uninstall any old antivirus software prior to installing McAfee.

Q: How do I reinstall McAfee on a replacement computer?

Log in to your existing McAfee account on the new device, navigate to the "My Account" dashboard, and click "Download" to transfer or reinstall your license.
